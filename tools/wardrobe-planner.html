Copy for Claude

Paste this into the chat. It lists every cupboard bottom to top with heights from the floor, and ends with a DATA line that holds the whole plan, so the drawings and the Blender model can be built straight from it.

How to use it

Cupboards

The strip at the top shows all seven bays as you see them standing in the room: the left wall L1–L3 (L1 by the bathroom door, L3 beside the mirror), and the right wall R4–R1 (R4 is the tunnel bay beside the mirror, R1 by the bedroom wall). Click one, or use the small plan, or [ ].

Name a cupboard on the right (Shoes, Linen, Daily…) and add a note if you like.

Fittings

Click a fitting in the list to drop it into the best free space, or drag it straight onto the drawing at the height you want. Every fitting starts at its usual real size.

Drag a fitting up or down to move it. It slides past others into the nearest space it fits and snaps to the 32 mm shelf-pin holes and to the fittings next to it. Hold ⌥ to place it freely.

Select it and drag the small tabs at its top and bottom to change its height. It can't go below its minimum (a hanging rail keeps its drop).

Checks

Red means it can't be built that way. Amber means it works but is awkward: a rail above easy reach, a drawer too high to see into, a shelf with too little space above it. Grey notes are just good to know.

The bar at the top right shows how much height is still free below the loft. The dashed boxes on the drawing are the free spaces.

Saving

Everything saves itself in this browser as you go. Export saves a file you can keep or open in another browser with Import. Undo and Redo go back and forth through every change. Reset can start again or load the 7 Oct layout.

Keys

⌫ delete · ⌘D duplicate · ↑ ↓ move one step (⇧ for 1 mm) · ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z undo / redo · Esc deselect.
